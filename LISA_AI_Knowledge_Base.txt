# COMPREHENSIVE FAQs (TECHNICAL & OPERATIONS)

### Q: Can Lisa AI speak languages other than English?
* **Answer:** Yes! Lisa AI supports multiple languages, including English, Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish, French, German, and more. She can easily match your target audience's language and accent.

### Q: Can we change or customize the voice?
* **Answer:** Yes. We use advanced voice synthesis (ElevenLabs). You can choose from pre-built natural voices, or we can clone your own voice (or your agent's voice) so it sounds exactly like you on the calls.

### Q: How does the AI handle interruptions? What if I talk over it?
* **Answer:** Lisa AI has near-zero latency and active interruption detection. If a prospect interrupts Lisa mid-sentence, she immediately stops talking, listens, and responds naturally, just like a human caller would.

### Q: Does it integrate with my existing CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Google Sheets)?
* **Answer:** Yes! We offer custom CRM integrations during the setup phase. All call recordings, transcripts, and interest tags can be pushed automatically to your current sales stack or CRM of choice.

### Q: Can we use our own business phone number as the Caller ID?
* **Answer:** Yes. We can verify and configure your existing business phone number so that when Lisa AI calls, clients see your brand name/number on their phone screen.

### Q: Will I be billed for busy lines or voicemails?
* **Answer:** No. Lisa AI operates on a pay-as-you-go minutes system. If a lead doesn't pick up, if the call hits an invalid number, or if it hangs up in under 5 seconds, it costs you $0 (or ₹0). You only pay for successful talk time.

### Q: Is our customer data safe and secure?
* **Answer:** Absolutely. Lisa AI is built with enterprise-level security. We use strict database isolation (Supabase Row-Level Security. Your lead sheets and customer details are entirely private to you.

### Q: How long does the setup take?
* **Answer:** We can set up your custom AI agent and launch your campaign within 24 to 78 hours once you submit the setup form.

### Q: Do we get free calling credits to test?
* **Answer:** Yes, every setup includes $10 (approx. ₹800) in free calling credits so you can run test calls and experience the setup before buying more credits.
